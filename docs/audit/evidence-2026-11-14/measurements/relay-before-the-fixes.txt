2. FIRST BOTTLENECK
The single event loop, first through the heartbeat: server.js:436-447 pings every socket in one synchronous loop every 30 s (:81). The loop alone costs 9.5 us per socket (95 ms at 10,000, 187 ms at 19,900, clients not answering); with pongs, 125-184 ms and 262-317 ms of CPU. A message relayed then waited 32-82 ms and 195-346 ms. Second is fan-out: broadcast() (:420-424) runs JSON.stringify per recipient (:416), rooms.js:64-74 copies the member list twice per message, and rooms are uncapped (rooms.js:26-37).

3. DEFECTS (all reproduced)
a. No backpressure. send() (server.js:414-418) never checks bufferedAmount; maxPayload is 256 KB (:58, :155) and the rate limit counts messages, not bytes. One recipient that stopped reading cost 404 MB in 60 s, freed only when the heartbeat terminated it. Fix: terminate a socket whose bufferedAmount passes 1 MB; cut MAX_MESSAGE_BYTES to 64 KB.
b. Fan-out amplification. One 250 KB chat into a 50-person room blocks the loop 43 ms; one socket inside the rate limit takes 89% of the core and pushes every other room to 0.26-0.76 s. Fix: stringify once in broadcast(); cap room size at join.
c. Heartbeat stall (:436-447). Fix: ping a few hundred sockets per tick.
d. The rate limit (240 per 10 s, :84-85) closes with 4008 instead of dropping (:277-281), and the close announces peer-left (:301-306), tearing down the peer connection. Reached by: pointer fallback at the web's 50 ms throttle (201 per window) plus an offer and 40 candidates; a newcomer answering 6 peers x 40 candidates (246) or 8 x 30 (248). A two-person connect (41) and pointers alone pass. Fix: drop the excess with an error.
e. Crash. server.js:158 new URL(request.url) throws on an upgrade whose path is "//"; the process exits and every socket drops (100 of 100). Unreachable through Apache's /signal prefix (300,000 fuzzed paths never throw); reachable by any local process on the shared server. Fix: try/catch, answer 400.
f. No connection cap (:157-201). At the fd ceiling (tested at a 1,024 limit) new sockets are accepted then reset, nothing is logged, and /health fails too (0 of 20); existing rooms keep working. Fix: answer 503 above a configured maximum, and log it.
Restart: shutdown (:462-475) closes everyone with 1001, emits no peer-left (0 seen), exits in 0.1-1.3 s. Each participant reconnects 350-650 ms later with a newly minted token: after an immediate restart all returned on the first attempt; after 5 s down 5,000 clients made 15,312 attempts, none gave up. No relay defect; the herd lands on the API.

4. NOT MEASURED
Beyond 19,900 sockets (fd limit); the Apache proxy and TLS; real network latency (clients shared the 4 cores, so heartbeat waits include their contention); the API's token-mint load; true CPU saturation; repeats of the single-run rows; presence at 19,900; whether churn's +18 MB RSS plateaus.
